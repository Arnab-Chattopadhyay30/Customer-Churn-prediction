This dataset contains details of a list of customers with the CustomerID, surname,Credit dcore,Geographic region, geneder etc. I performed a prediction of the customer whether they are excited or not on the basis of the whether they have card, how many products they purchased, how much balance they have and all. I created a model to predict the desired prediction. This is a supervised learning model, so the data was previously labeled. To perform the job first I checked that whether the data is balanced or not on the basis of that I performed the Exploratory Data Analysis. SMOTE is one of the best approach to perform the EDA on this dataset. During this process I found that the data was imbalanced, So I needed to balance the data first, so that I could escape the overfitting or underfitting issue.
Here I split the dataset into train and test dataset, the ratio of the splitting was 80:20 respectively and the splittng was performed randomly, means the order of the trainning and testing data was picked up randomly.
The model selection process started by the SVM model, here all the default parameters were used like the kernel was used "rbf", gamma was "scale", max_iter was 10000, on the basis of that this model gave the accuracy of 0.8035.
Then I used RnadomForest Classifier, here also I used the default or standard parameters, on that basis I found the accuracy of 0.8646.
Then my approach was the use of Decision Tree Classifier, where the accuracy was 0.784.
By applying all the models I found that RandomForest Classifier model gave the highest accuracy in this dataset.
